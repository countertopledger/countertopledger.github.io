How the score works

Each appliance gets points from your answers: +3 for each thing you want to make that it does, +2 if its size suits your household, +2 if it suits your space, +1 if it suits your level. We list the top match first. This is our own rule of thumb, not a lab result. We do not test products and show no prices, because prices change daily.

Capacity and model facts are the ones in our Black Friday kitchen guide; the size, space and level fit is our own call. Version 0 covers five appliances; more are coming.
