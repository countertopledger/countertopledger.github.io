About Countertop Ledger

We help people buy coffee and kitchen gear with confidence: what's worth buying, and when to buy it.

How we choose what to recommend

  • We focus on three areas: coffee and espresso, small appliances, and cookware.
  • We look at what a product does well, who it suits, and what people who own it report.
  • Brands cannot pay to be included or to change what we say.

Price tracking: how it will work

We are building a daily price history for popular coffee and kitchen products. Once we have enough history, each product page will show whether today's price is low, normal or high compared with the last 90 days, and you'll be able to sign up for an email when a price drops.
