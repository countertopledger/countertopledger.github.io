The short answer: which kitchen deals are worth waiting for? For Black Friday kitchen deals, the usual candidates are a stand mixer (KitchenAid mixer deals are the classic), a multicooker like the Instant Pot, an air fryer, an espresso machine and a good Dutch oven. Black Friday 2026 kitchen appliances in these categories are often marked down, but not every "deal" is one: advertised percentages usually start from the list price, not from what the item sold for lately. Below are five picks to watch and a 3-step check you can do in about a minute. If something you rely on every day just broke, buy a replacement now; early deals are already live.

Dates: Amazon says Black Friday Week runs Nov 19–30, 2026, so deals have been live since November 19. Black Friday itself is November 27 and Cyber Monday is November 30, but you do not have to wait for either day. For gifts, order by about December 18 for Christmas delivery.

How to tell if it's a real deal
  1. Compare with the last few months, not the crossed-out price. Many Amazon product pages show a price history. A real deal is lower than the item has recently been, not just lower than the list price.
  2. Match the exact model. Sale versions can be a different size or a stripped-down model with a similar name. Compare the model number or size we list for each pick.
  3. Be wary of prices that only appear in the cart or after clipping a coupon. Judge the final price you would pay, with any bundle you don't need left out.

What to skip

  • Mystery-brand doorbusters. A huge "percent off" on a brand you have never heard of often starts from an inflated list price. Stick to brands you know, and match the exact model number.
  • Bundles you don't need. Extra lids, recipe books and accessory kits make a deal look bigger without adding much.
  • Waiting for something you need today. If your only coffee maker or toaster just died, the savings rarely justify weeks without it.

Black Friday or Cyber Monday? Pick a rule, not a day

Amazon's Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deal events run through November 30, so you do not have to bet on one date. Instead of guessing which day will be better, decide in advance what makes you buy:

  • Buy when the price clears your own bar. Note the price you see now, decide what drop would make you act, and buy whenever the exact model reaches it, whether that is Black Friday, Cyber Monday or a day in between.
  • Buy now if you need it now. If a missing appliance is costing you time or money every day, a decent deal today beats a possible better one later.
  • Skip if the price never clears your bar. Missing the sale costs you nothing if the item was not on your list.

Before you buy, look for the price history link next to the price on the exact model's Amazon page (Amazon shows it on eligible product pages). If today's price is not lower than it has recently been, wait or skip.
