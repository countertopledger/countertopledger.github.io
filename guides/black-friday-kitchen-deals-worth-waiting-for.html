What to skip

  • Mystery-brand doorbusters. A huge "percent off" on a brand you have never heard of often starts from an inflated list price. Stick to models with a long rating history.
  • Bundles you don't need. Extra lids, recipe books and accessory kits make a deal look bigger without adding much.
  • Waiting for something you need today. If your only coffee maker or toaster just died, the savings rarely justify weeks without it.
