Gifts for coffee lovers, from small to splurge

Six gifts that a coffee person will use every morning, ordered from stocking stuffer to big upgrade, with what to look for in each.

Timing tip: coffee gear usually sees its deepest discounts from Black Friday (November 27) to Cyber Monday (November 30). Order by mid-December for holiday delivery.

Handheld milk frother

Café-style foam for lattes at home. Look for a rechargeable one with a stand. Our pick: the Maestri House LunaFro, which charges by USB and comes with a stand.

Coffee scale with timer

The cheapest way to make every cup taste the same. Look for 0.1 g precision and a built-in timer. Our pick: the Greater Goods coffee scale, simple and accurate.

Usually $20–60See our pick on Amazon

Pour-over set

A dripper and carafe for a slower, cleaner cup. Glass or ceramic both work well. Our pick: the Bodum 34 oz pour-over, with a reusable steel filter, so no paper to buy.

Usually $25–60See our pick on Amazon

Gooseneck electric kettle

Precise pouring for pour-over and tea. Variable temperature control is worth paying for. Our pick: the Cosori gooseneck kettle, with presets for coffee and tea.

Usually $40–150See our pick on Amazon

Burr grinder

The upgrade that changes taste the most. Choose burrs over blades, and check the grind range fits how they brew. Our pick: the OXO Brew conical burr grinder, a reliable first grinder.

Usually $100–300See our pick on Amazon

Home espresso machine

The big splurge. Beginners do best with a semi-automatic machine that has a steam wand. Our pick: the Breville Bambino Plus, which heats up in seconds and froths milk automatically.

Usually $300 and upSee our pick on Amazon

How to pick

  • Already has a grinder? Go for a scale or kettle. They improve every cup for little money.
  • Drinks lattes? A frother is the safe small gift; an espresso machine is the big one.
  • Not sure how they brew? A scale works with every method.
