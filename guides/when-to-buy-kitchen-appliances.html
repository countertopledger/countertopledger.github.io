The four sale windows that matter

1. Black Friday to Cyber Monday (late November)

This is when manufacturers and retailers cut prices the most on small kitchen appliances. In 2026 it runs from November 27 to November 30, and many deals start weeks earlier.

2. After Christmas into January

Holiday leftovers get marked down, and "new year, new you" promotions push blenders and air fryers.

3. Amazon's summer Prime Day

One of the biggest days of the year for small kitchen appliances. It is usually in July; in 2026 it ran in June.

4. Amazon's October sale (Prime Big Deal Days)

A second Prime event ahead of the holidays. In 2026 it ran October 6 and 7.

Is it worth waiting?

  • If you need it now, buy it. Holiday weekends like Presidents' Day, Memorial Day and Labor Day bring smaller discounts that still help.
  • If it's a big purchase like a stand mixer or espresso machine, and late November is a few weeks away, waiting usually pays off.
  • Consider certified refurbished models for premium brands. They are often much cheaper and come with a warranty.
