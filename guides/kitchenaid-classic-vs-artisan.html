KitchenAid Classic vs Artisan: what's actually different

Both are tilt-head KitchenAid stand mixers. KitchenAid's own comparison lists a handful of differences: bowl size, motor, a pouring shield and colors.

The short answer: on KitchenAid's own comparison, the Artisan has a 5-quart bowl with a handle, a 325-watt motor, a pouring shield in the box and more than 20 colors. The Classic has a 4.5-quart bowl without a handle, a 275-watt motor and comes in black or white. Everything else KitchenAid lists is the same: tilt-head design, 10 speeds, the attachment hub, the included beater, dough hook and whip, the listed dimensions and the warranty. If those extras matter to you, look at the Artisan. If they do not, the Classic is the same style of mixer.

Who should skip each one

KitchenAid Classic (K45SS)

Skip it if you bake a few times a year, or you have no cabinet or counter spot for it; a hand mixer may be enough. Also skip it if you want a pouring shield in the box, a bowl with a handle, or a color other than black or white. Those are the Artisan's extras.

We have not confirmed a single Amazon product page for the K45SS yet, so there is no button here. If you shop for it, match the model number K45SS on the page you open.

KitchenAid Artisan (KSM150PS)

Skip it if you bake a few times a year, or you have no cabinet or counter spot for it; a hand mixer may be enough. Also skip it if the extra half quart and 50 watts would not change what you make, since the listed size, speeds and attachment hub are the same as the Classic's.

Before you pick one

  • Check the exact model number. The Classic is K45SS and the Artisan is KSM150PS. Match the number on the page you open before you buy.
  • Measure the spot. KitchenAid lists the same three dimensions for both mixers but does not label which is the height. Look at the product page for the model you choose, then measure the cabinet or counter space.
  • Compare the price with the recent past. Note the price you see now, then look for the price history link next to the price on the exact model's Amazon page (Amazon shows it on eligible product pages). If today's price is not lower than it has recently been, wait or skip.
